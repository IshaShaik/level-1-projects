What is a sample? One example of a pose that the AI uses during training — a snapshot of your body's landmark positions, not the video frame itself.
What is a class? The name/category assigned to a pose, like "Tree Pose" or "Warrior Pose".
Why MediaPipe? It detects shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles, so the model learns from body structure instead of raw pixels — much easier to understand and far less data-hungry.
