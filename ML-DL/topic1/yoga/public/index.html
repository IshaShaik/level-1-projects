How the workflow fits together

1
Create a project, then add a pose class for each pose you want to teach it.
2
Open your camera and capture samples while you hold each pose — MediaPipe reads your body landmarks in real time.
3
Once every pose has enough samples, train a small neural network right in the browser.
4
Test it live: strike a pose and watch the confidence bars respond.
5
Explore your model, export it, or export the raw training data to keep learning in Python.
Camera processing happens locally in your browser. Video is never uploaded — only landmark coordinates and small thumbnails are saved to your project.
